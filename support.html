A little guidance.

Contact Yucong Du at yucongdu@yahoo.com. Include your iPhone model and app version if reporting a problem. Please do not include private worries, passwords or payment details.

How do I use Throw It Away?

Tap the paper to write, or tap a suggested thought to start an editable draft. Press and move your finger to crumple the paper, then swipe to let go. Every throw succeeds.

I cannot hear the sounds.

Turn on Sound in Settings and check your iPhone media volume and audio output. Adjust Environment, Paper and Throw & release separately. Sound can play in Silent Mode. Headphones are recommended.

What does Complete Experience include?

A single optional purchase permanently removes banner ads and unlocks The Volcano, The Ocean and Snowfall. It is not a subscription. The US price is $2.99; the App Store displays the price for your region before purchase.

How do I restore a purchase?

Use the same Apple Account that made the purchase, open Settings in the app, and choose Restore purchases. An internet connection may be needed. TestFlight purchases are for testing and do not transfer to the public App Store app.

Where are my words saved?

Your text is held only in the current app session. The app does not save it to a journal, send it to a server or share it with advertising services. Letting go clears the ritual text; closing the app ends the session. Your keyboard provider or iOS may have its own data practices.

Can I change advertising privacy choices?

Where applicable, choose Ad privacy choices in Settings. Banners are shown only outside the writing and release ritual. Complete Experience removes ads.

Can I use easier controls?

Enable Simple controls in Settings for Crumple and Throw buttons. The app also responds to VoiceOver and the system Reduce Motion setting.
