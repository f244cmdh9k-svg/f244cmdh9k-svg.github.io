# Throw It Away
## A tactile ritual for letting go

**First public release · iPhone · Created by Yucong Du**

A thought becomes a handwritten-style note. A fingertip turns it into a paper ball. A swipe sends it away. Throw It Away makes that short sequence the center of the experience, pairing responsive paper sounds with haptics and atmospheric scenes.

### The editorial story

The idea starts with an everyday action: writing something down and crumpling the paper. Here, the user supplies the meaning. It might be an unfinished task, an awkward conversation, or simply tomorrow's alarm. Tappable suggestions help people find a starting point, and every suggestion becomes an editable draft.

The interaction invites expression without requiring precision. A gentle movement or a quicker crumple changes the paper feedback. The throw always reaches its destination. There is no score, leaderboard, level or failed attempt between the user and the ending.

Sound gives the gesture its character. Layered paper recordings respond to movement while the background recedes. A room ends with the paper landing in a bin; a furnace with fire; the void with a specially mixed absorption sound that sits within its musical background. Moving firelight and the black hole's moving outer glow carry the atmosphere beyond a static backdrop.

### What to experience first

1. In My Room, tap a suggestion and edit it to “Tomorrow can wait.” Hear the paper while crumpling slowly, then more quickly, before throwing it into the bin.
2. Try The Furnace and The Void to compare the moving environments and their distinct endings. Headphones reveal the paper texture and the changing balance between foreground and background sound.
3. In Settings, try Simple controls and adjust Environment, Paper and release audio separately. VoiceOver support and Reduce Motion provide further ways to use the ritual.
