The idea

One thought. One sheet.
One small moment of release.

A deadline. A conversation on repeat. Tomorrow’s alarm. Type what is on your mind, or tap a suggestion and make it your own. Your words appear on a sheet of paper, ready to crumple and throw.

Paper sounds respond to your movement as the background gently recedes. Then comes a different ending in each space: a familiar bin, moving firelight, a cosmic void, lava, ocean waves or snowfall. There is no score or perfect aim to chase.

About the preview

The preview follows three paper rituals: a handwritten-style note crumples into a ball and lands in a bedroom bin, burns in a furnace, then disappears into a black hole. It shows actual native app footage using the available Simple controls mode. The soundtrack uses the app’s bundled audio, mixed and synchronized to the recording.
